# Local access normalization policy — 2026-08-12

User preference clarified during Fujian gateway convergence: short local taxi-equivalent movement should not affect comparative trip cost or time because a conventional same-city itinerary also requires airport/port access.

Policy effect:

- short local first/last-mile or intra-gateway-area movement normally handled by taxi or ordinary local transit is excluded from comparative cost;
- the same short local movement is excluded from comparative transport time and usable-time deduction;
- exact fare/minutes for that class are not required and cannot fail-close a candidate by themselves;
- practical connection feasibility is still required when the local movement feeds a fixed ferry/flight/rail departure;
- intercity/cross-region transfer, ferry, rail, domestic air, check-in/waiting, and transport-caused overnight remain material and must still be normalized/revalidated.

For the current Fujian case this means KNH↔Shuitou and Wutong↔Xiamen local access are no longer cost/time blockers. Huangqi↔Fuzhou remains material because it is an intercity transfer, and the Beigan transport-caused overnight remains material.
